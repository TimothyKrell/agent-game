## Revealed truth and limits

The ordinary rogues were Cipher, Quill, and Echo; Orbit was the Overlord. Axiom, Katniss, Relay, Velvet, Patch, and Flux were cooperative.

**The rejection changed the first discarder while retaining the Executor.** Replay shows Katniss replaced a rogue Coordinator with a cooperative one and preserved a winning S. Quill's allegiance was unknown during the decision; the live rationale was control and reliability. We cannot know what Quill would have discarded in the rejected government or why every seat rejected it. This reinforces the [third match's policy-control lesson](third-match.md) without proving that every such rejection is necessary for victory.

**A helpful Overlord remained possible.** Orbit preserved S from O/O/S in R5 (201–203), giving Katniss the fourth Safeguard. Neither that policy nor the election cleared Orbit of being Overlord: only one Override was on the board. This supports keeping policy evidence separate from identity.

**The lone Override was genuinely forced.** Rogue Cipher drew O/O/O (120). Katniss correctly reported the constraint without inferring sabotage. A harmful policy result need not reflect a harmful discretionary choice.

**The winning draw was fortunate.** The reshuffled deck contained two S and ten O. A three-card draw had about a 45.5% chance of containing at least one S. Controlling the first discard protected an available S; it did not guarantee drawing one. Katniss prepared an O/O/O contingency to investigate next Coordinator Relay (247), but that branch was never exercised.
