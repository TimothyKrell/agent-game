## Rules and authority

Reuse the original engine through a narrow current-board/event-append seam. Do not duplicate legislation/executive rules or clone accumulated history for Succession. Intercept all four child wins: fifth Safeguard, sixth Override, late Overlord election, and Overlord execution (including self-execution and chaos track wins). Replace child match-complete/reveal events with act-qualified facts. Child interruption interrupts the whole match.

Persist the final Act 1 move, transition, all-ten return, coin assignment, fresh shuffle/deal, receipt, new decision fences and appended facts in one SQLite transaction. Never publish an intermediate terminal match, release its allocation, or settle Act 1. Keep roster, admission, mode, creation time, starting ratings and controller authority. Prior forfeit/generation/house authority persists. Reset chat cooldowns; no resurrection reauthorization.

Publish historical Act 1 roles, faction result, returned markers and bonuses at transition. Keep private policy/investigation history entitled until overall termination. Initial Act 2 actor is independent uniform randomness in the original seat ring. The 25-card deck contains five copies of each capability; hands have two cards, court five. Proof, replacement, chosen loss, exchange and sealed responses follow the complete public rules. Cards and nonnegative coins are conserved according to those rules; integrity failure interrupts without an invented fallback.

Each complete match has one mechanical champion. Credited win is `seat === winnerSeat && !forfeited`; a forfeited champion yields zero credited wins. Relief controllers are not extra entrants. One overall settlement, one participant row per original entrant. Preview/evaluation and interruption do not alter ratings or placements.

## Protocol 2, privacy and bounded history

Current observations are complete, separate from history, and at most 14,336 UTF-8 JSON bytes; envelopes at most 16,384. At most 32 choices, labels at most 80 bytes using seat numbers. Events at most 8,192 bytes, including valid 1,000-code-point maximum Unicode/control-escaped chat. History pages use at most 64 indexed rows; requested count 1–64 (default 32), bytes 12,288–32,768 (default 16,384). Metadata at most 1,024 bytes. Current/checkpoint rows at most 65,536 bytes; replay frames at most 32,768 bytes. Never truncate required choices or accepted history to meet bounds.

Current carries only `{visibilityEpoch, streamHead}` for history. Pages use epoch, exclusive `after`, frozen inclusive `through`, actual delivered `cursor`, events and `hasMore`. IDs are contiguous within entitlement; opaque immutable `eventKey` supports anchors across terminal renumbering. Validate capability/auth before epoch; mismatch returns metadata-only reset with empty events and zero cursor. Only consumed matching-epoch/matching-after pages advance delivered history. Head growth is availability, not consumption. Socket/current/receipt cannot advance history delivery.

Append canonical events and indexed public/seat sequence mappings/heads atomically with board, receipt and outbox. Retired-controller entitlement is retained pre-takeover private prefix plus future public tail, using persisted cutoffs; house receives seat entitlement. Live history excludes replay-only facts. Terminal archive uses canonical sequence with a durable new epoch, without full-log rewrite. Current load/evolution reads zero historical rows. No unbounded invisible-prefix scan, OFFSET or recount. House recent prompts use bounded indexed facts plus bounded notebook. Incremental private checkpoints/rule indexes permit replay at each cursor with one checkpoint and at most 64 rule facts; chat does not require board re-evolution. Entire two-act archive remains retrievable page by page.

Unsolicited delivery occurs only when recipient-entitled current state or epoch/head changes. Private sealed submissions must cause no identical/surrogate-changed frames for uninvolved seats/spectators. Fixed heartbeat and explicit resync are allowed. Public grace can reveal an outstanding choice and takeover identifies a nonresponder; ordinary reaction values/counts/order remain sealed. No mutation timestamps, ETags, revisions or phase IDs expose submission counts.

**C1:** delayed HTTP current, receipt or old-connection snapshot cannot replace later terminal/archive/entitlement state. Correlate match/audience/request/connection generation; acknowledge receipt identity separately from its attached snapshot. Reject obsolete pages. ## Cumulative automatic-play lifecycle

D14's **120-minute cumulative Succession match / 10-minute cumulative queue / at-most-10-minute healthy child** default was adopted by the coordinator on 2026-09-13. This is a bounded client resource profile, not a game-completion guarantee. Secret Overlord retains its omitted 35-minute numeric match default. A legal 120-turn theft/block/pass path approaches 4h20 in Act 2 alone and 2,400 required submissions. The default stops honestly at 120 minutes; the server continues and missing a required decision may cause controller forfeiture. An explicit larger runtime allowance is supported without increasing inference budgets. The zero-cost evidence distinguishes 250 actual-engine simulations (maximum virtual duration 29m47s, excluding model/network latency, chat, grace and recovery) from the injected 260-minute supervisor timeline (120-minute default stops after 12 invocations; explicit 300-minute allowance completes the injected path after 26).
